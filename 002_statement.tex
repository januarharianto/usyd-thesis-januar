\section*{Author Attribution}

The work contained in the body of this thesis, except otherwise acknowledged, is the result of my own investigations. 

\textbf{Chapter \ref{chapter2}} of this thesis is \href{https://doi.org/10/gdqdv4}{published} in \textit{Marine Biology}. The experiment was designed and conducted by me (JH) with help from Sebastian Holmes (SH) and Maria Byrne (MB). Hong Dao Nguyen (HDN) assisted with the collection of samples for protein analysis and SH helped with the experimental design of respirometry analyses. The manuscript was written by JH but all co-authors contributed to the drafts.

\textbf{Chapter \ref{chapter3}} was an extensive experiment spanning a few months. The co-authors of this study include Sergio Torres-Gabarda (STG), Joshua Aldridge (JA), Richard Grainger (RG), MB, and JH. In addition, valuable lessons were learned from Nicholas Carey (NC), which allowed JH to improve the collection of respirometry data. RG contributed to the collection of feeding rate data, while STG and JA took turns with JH in data collection and maintenance of the elaborate experiment. MB provided important insights to the experimental design and contributed greatly to manuscript drafts.

\textbf{Chapter \ref{chapter4}} was performed by JH, STG, JA and MB. JH designed the experiment and conducted all the analyses. STG and JA were again indispensable -- they audited the experiment constantly to prevent catastrophic failure, and contributed to the construction of the larval incubation system. MB assisted in the collection of data from the larvae/juveniles. All co-authors have reviewed drafts for the manuscript in preparation for submission.

\textbf{Chapter \ref{chapter5}} of this thesis has been submitted into \textit{Methods in Ecology and Evolution}. It is currently undergoing a \textit{third} review. NC and JH spent many sleepless nights programming wonderful and insane ideas for the R package and writing multiple manuscript drafts. MB provided vital feedback in the writing of the manuscript.
