\chapter{Preface}

The data chapters presented in this thesis are written in a format for publication in peer-reviewed, scientific journals. 
Because they have been prepared as stand-alone manuscripts, repetition of important information was unavoidable and some chapter introductions may appear redundant to the General Introduction (Chapter 1).
In addition, table/figure legends and citations have been re-formatted and compiled at the end of the thesis to assist in easier cross-referencing.
Because the thesis is digital, I have enabled hyperlinks in the document for citations, cross-references and geographic locations. 
These links are obvious -- they are blue in colour.
Clicking on a citation will bring the reader directly to the relevant reference; clicking on a figure or table reference will skip the reader to the page containing that figure/table; and clicking on a location will automatically launch a web browser showing that exact location on Google Maps.
If the reader is using a PDF reader with ""back/forward"" functionality, activating the "back" function will always bring the reader back to the same page before a link was clicked.
I hope that these small, but useful additions to the PDF, will help the reader in navigating the thesis in a manner that is more enjoyable.
